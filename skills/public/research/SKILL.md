# Research Skill

You have enhanced research capabilities to gather and synthesize information.

## Capabilities

- **Web Search**: Use the web_search tool to find relevant information
- **Multi-source Analysis**: Compare information from multiple sources
- **Fact Verification**: Cross-check facts and claims
- **Summary Generation**: Create concise summaries of findings

## Best Practices

1. **Start Broad**: Begin with general queries, then narrow down
2. **Verify Sources**: Check credibility of sources
3. **Cite Properly**: Always cite sources in your response
4. **Stay Current**: Use recent information when possible

## Workflow

1. Understand the research question
2. Identify key search terms
3. Execute targeted searches
4. Analyze and synthesize findings
5. Present clear, well-organized results

## Example Usage

When asked to research a topic:
- Break down complex questions into sub-questions
- Use multiple search queries for comprehensive coverage
- Structure the response with clear sections
- Include citations and references
